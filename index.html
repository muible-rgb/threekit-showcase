Frequently asked questions about AI guided selling.

What marketing, digital, IT, and sales leaders ask most when evaluating Threekit.

What is AI guided selling?
AI guided selling is a buying experience where an AI agent - trained on the manufacturer's real catalog and business rules - asks questions, guides, and recommends the right product. It replaces filters, endless website scrolling, brochures, and static forms. The same agent works for end customers, dealers, pros, and internal sales teams.
Which B2B manufacturers use Threekit?
All of our customers work with Threekit for the same reason: to be easier to do business with. They sell complex products, large catalogs, or both - and they use AI guided selling and visualization to take friction out of the buying experience for end customers, dealers, and their own sales teams.
How does the Threekit agent qualify a buyer?
The agent asks the buyer a short set of questions tied to the catalog - job, facility type, application, traffic load, style preference. It uses those answers to narrow to a valid configuration and routes the qualified lead to the dealer or sales team with the configuration attached. Average lead value goes up by 50% on Threekit-engaged pipeline.
What kind of revenue impact does Threekit deliver?
Customers see significant top-line lift. An enterprise door manufacturer added $20M+ in pipeline value. A large commercial plumbing manufacturer drove $40M in incremental sales. Ulrich Lifestyle Structures saw a 290% revenue increase and 400% increase in units sold within a month of launch.
How long does it take to launch a Threekit AI agent?
Implementation is usually 1-3 months, depending on catalog complexity and integrations. Engagements typically kick off with a short working session - bring your catalog, leave with an AI agent built on it. The agent is built directly on your Threekit catalog and business rules.
How does this fit with my IT systems and tech stack?
Threekit is built to layer onto whatever you already run. The agent lives on your site and connects into your existing eCommerce, CRM, CPQ, PIM, and order systems - Salesforce, Shopify, SAP Commerce Cloud, commercetools, Magento, BigCommerce, and custom stacks all work. The configured output flows back into your systems of record so reps, dealers, and ops see the same source of truth.
